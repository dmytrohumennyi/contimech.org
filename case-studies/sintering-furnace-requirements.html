Requirements baseline for a continuous sintering furnace

An anonymised reconstruction for a synthetic powder-metallurgy line: a continuous sintering furnace with an N₂/H₂ protective atmosphere. It shows how we go from operating scenarios and hazards to allocated system, hardware and software requirements, each with a verification method.

Type
Public reference, synthetic line
Scope
68 requirements: system, HW, SW
Methods
Operating scenarios, HAZOP, safety decomposition, verification plan
Services
2.2 Requirements · 1.2 Analysis

Task

The starting point was a business problem: scrap rates that varied from week to week. The analysis followed one chain from that problem through process observability and atmosphere hazards to system requirements, their allocation to hardware and software, and the tests that would prove them.

What the requirements changed

Several findings changed the design, not just the paperwork:

  • The original interlock proved temperature only in the hottest zone, where the hazard is least present. Atmosphere conditions are now proven per zone below the ignition threshold.
  • The emergency stop closed the normally-closed atmosphere valves and cut protective gas while the muffle was hot and contained hydrogen. Stop functions were separated.
  • Belt motion was sensed on the motor shaft, which cannot see a slipping coupling. The encoder moves to the driven pulley.
  • Process records were attached to a batch by wall-clock time at the exit, mixing up conditions from the wrong hour. Records are now associated by belt position and dwell time.

Sample requirements

SNTR-SYS-ATM-020The system shall prove the atmosphere enabling condition separately for each zone whose nominal operating temperature is below the safe ignition temperature.Verification: zonal interlock test
SNTR-SYS-SAF-020Actuation of the emergency stop shall not remove the means of maintaining the atmosphere safe state.Verification: emergency-stop test at temperature with atmosphere state monitored
SNTR-HWR-SAF-020Belt motion shall be sensed by an encoder on the driven pulley, not on the motor shaft.Verification: inspection and coupling-release fault injection
SNTR-SWR-REC-010The software shall associate recorded process conditions with a batch by belt position and dwell time, not by wall-clock time at the exit.Verification: batch transit and recording correlation test
